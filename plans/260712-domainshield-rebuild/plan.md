# DomainShield Rebuild Plan

## Visual direction

DomainShield uses a domain-intelligence cartography system: warm white, near-black,
ultramarine, vermilion risk marks, DNS-grid lines, domain labels, and evidence paths.
It must not reuse ModDAO's left operations rail or NeighborPeace's civic editorial
layout.

## Pages

- `/`: domain protection overview and contract connection.
- `/how-it-works`: policy, evidence, comparative jury, appeal, and payout guide.
- `/setup`: initialize contract.
- `/reserve`: add funds and inspect reserve state.
- `/policies`: on-chain policy list.
- `/policies/new`: create policy.
- `/claims`: on-chain claim list.
- `/claims/new`: file claim.
- `/claims/[id]`: claim evidence and decision detail.
- `/claims/[id]/review`: run AI evaluation.
- `/claims/[id]/appeal`: submit one appeal.
- `/claims/[id]/appeal/review`: evaluate appeal evidence.
- `/claims/[id]/payout`: finalize approved payout ledger.
- `/activity`: live contract sync and Explorer proof.

Forms use a domain map/evidence packet interaction rather than the generic stacked
card used by earlier projects. Long pages reveal sections once on scroll and respect
reduced-motion preferences.
